W# VHDL & Digital Design

Hi, I'm **Mubinur Rashid Tahjib** 👋

I'm an electronics engineer currently doing an **M2 in Integrated Circuits and Systems** at **Université Paris-Saclay**. My background is in **embedded hardware design**, and I'm now going deeper into **digital design, FPGAs and integrated circuits**.

This repository collects my **VHDL designs**, from course practical sessions and personal practice. Each one is simulated and verified with testbenches.

---

## 🔧 What I work on

- **PCB & embedded hardware design**: multi-layer boards, high-speed interfaces (MIPI, LPDDR4, eMMC, PCIe), BLE / Wi-Fi devices, motor control drivers
- **Digital design & FPGA**: VHDL, RTL design, testbenches, clock domain crossing, Intel (Altera) FPGAs with Quartus
- **IC design (coursework)**: standard-cell design in Cadence Virtuoso, arithmetic operators for digital computing
- **Power electronics**: DC-DC converter and inverter topologies; my undergraduate thesis was on breakdown voltage enhancement in GaN HEMTs
- **Machine learning (side work)**: time-series forecasting of electrical load
